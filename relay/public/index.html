How the agents coordinate

Each Cubbie is its student’s agent. Agents never message each other’s students. They talk through the circle relay and only ever ask their own person.

1

Ranked times

The proposing Cubbie sends up to five windows, best first: before the shared exam, sooner first.

2

Busy check

The other Cubbie checks its own student’s calendar privately and answers for them: free, or busy.

3

Next common time

On busy, the relay moves the same plan to the next time free for everyone, skipping any already turned down.

4

Max 4 rounds

If nothing fits, it stops with “no shared time this week” instead of nagging anyone.

What agents share

  • A busy level (light, busy, packed) and big-day dates. Never the reason.
  • Coarse free windows like thu-evening. Never event names or exact times.
  • Course codes with exam and deadline dates, visible only to friends in the same course.

When students confirm

  • Before a proposal is sent.
  • Before accepting a time, and only when their own calendar is free.
  • Before anything lands on their calendar. Cubbie never texts your friends. You send the texts.

Watchable circles

Anyone with a circle’s code can watch it work, live, without joining. It only happens when a member turns it on.

Viewers see

  • Names and a busy level for each member.
  • Plans with their status, date, time, place and why that time was picked.
  • The agent log, line by line, as the Cubbies talk.

Viewers never see

  • Free times or exact calendar entries.
  • Course lists beyond the plan’s own course.
  • Messages to friends, or any keys and tokens.
Turn it on by texting Cubbie “make my circle watchable”. Cubbie explains what viewers can see and asks yes or no first. Text it again to turn it off.

Private by construction

These limits are enforced by the server, not left to the app’s good behavior.
